How we work

We may not always have a role open. We always have room for the right person.

Project based

We build a small team around each client. Most roles are freelance or contract, booked project by project.

Montreal and remote

Shoots happen in Montreal. Design, editing and strategy can be done from anywhere in Canada or the US.

Founder led

You work directly with Helia. Every application is read personally, and we reach out when a project fits you.
