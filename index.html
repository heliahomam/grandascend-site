01 Who We Are

A creative house for visionary brands.

GrandAscend is a Montreal-based creative marketing agency specializing in luxury and beauty branding. Founded by LVMH-certified strategist Helia Homam, the agency brings together brand strategy, content creation, and Shopify design to help ambitious businesses rise above the noise.

Every brand has a story worth telling. We help you find it, shape it, and share it with the world through strategy, design, and content that actually connects.
